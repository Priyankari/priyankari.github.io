I'm a PhD student at the University of Maryland. I am fortunate to be advised by [Ritwik Gupta](https://ritwikgupta.me/). My research interests are broadly in representation learning and geospatial foundation models, and I am motivated to apply this research to disaster response.

Previously, I worked as a federal contractor at the Army Research Laboratory. I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Texas A&M University, where I was fortunate to be advised by Robin Murphy and was part of the team that developed the [CLARKE](https://www.cmu.edu/ai-sdm/research/research-highlights/bda-rda-models.html) system for disaster response, which was deployed in response to Hurricanes Helene and Debby.
